Reality Mode

Stop choosing arbitrary numbers. Ask what the universe actually supplies.

Reality Mode grounds the capture experiment in observed or literature-based neutron-star speeds, cluster environments, coherent stellar mass/radius presets, gravitational focusing and a tidal-energy model. Then it asks: how much of plausible parameter space actually captures?

Sources & model notes

NS velocities: classic isolated-pulsar Maxwellian σ=265 km/s, mean 3-D speed ≈420 km/s; modern binary-NS kick work finds mode ≈74 and mean ≈97 km/s. Hobbs summary · binary-kick study.

Clusters: dense globular benchmark σ1D≈10 km/s and n≈10⁵ pc⁻³; YMCs are ≳10⁴ M☉, compact and can reach high densities, with observed young-cluster dispersions around 10–20 km/s. globular benchmark · YMC properties.

Tidal capture: Press–Teukolsky energy-transfer form and n=3 coupling fit from Generozov et al.. Linear/polytropic theory remains an approximation.

IMF: Kroupa-like α=1.3 below 0.5 M☉ and α=2.3 above. summary.

Cen X‑3: recent parameter tables support the values shown above. 2026 XRISM table · system parameters.

Reality Mode v1.0 is a literature-grounded exploratory calculation, not a peer-reviewed population-synthesis model. Its job is to make the remaining assumptions explicit and testable.
