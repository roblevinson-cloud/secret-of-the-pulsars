Cen X‑3 Reverse Engineering Mode

Start with what exists. Work backward to the capture encounter.

Cen X‑3 gives us a hard endpoint: a massive O-star donor and a neutron star in an extraordinarily tight, nearly circular 2.087-day orbit. This page asks which hypothetical free-neutron-star encounters can capture, what first bound orbit they create, and how much additional orbital evolution is required to approach the system we actually observe.

5 · What this calculation assumes

Energy → first bound orbit

E∞=½μv∞². The page computes Press–Teukolsky-style tidal deposition. If ΔE_tide>E∞, then a_post=GM★MNS/[2(ΔE_tide−E∞)] and approximates e_post≈1−rₚ/a_post.

First bound orbit → circular orbit

The page uses a_circ≈a_post(1−e_post²), the circular radius under approximate orbital-angular-momentum conservation, then compares that with the observed 18.14 R☉. Any mismatch is shown as an explicit remaining evolution requirement.
